Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Platform: Minsplay Streaming

Minsplay ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, processing, third-party infrastructure, and your privacy rights under GDPR, CCPA, and Google/Meta Platform Safety standards.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide low-latency vertical streaming and account synchronization:

  • Account Data: Display name, email address, and avatar provided via Google or Facebook OAuth.
  • Engagement & Wallet: Coin balances, daily check-in streaks, unlocked episode IDs, and watchlist history.
  • User-Generated Content: Episodic comments, likes, and creator applications.
  • Technical Telemetry: IP address, device type, operating system version, and streaming buffer diagnostics.

2. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or UK, our legal bases for processing your data include:

  • Contractual Performance: To maintain your active sessions, deliver purchased episodes, and sync wallet coins.
  • Legitimate Interests: To detect streaming abuse, moderate creator uploads, and optimize CDN delivery.
  • Consent: For opt-in features such as promotional alerts and personalization.

3. California Consumer Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have specific statutory rights:

  • Right to Know & Access: Request disclosure of the personal data categories collected.
  • Right to Delete: Request complete deletion of your profile and data records.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Equal service pricing and quality regardless of rights exercised.
  • Notice of Non-Sale: We do not sell or share your personal information for monetary or commercial cross-context advertising.

4. Cookies & Local Client Storage

Minsplay uses browser storage and essential cookies strictly to maintain user authentication tokens, video progress timestamps, and playback audio preferences. We do not use third-party tracking pixels.

5. Cloud Infrastructure & Third Parties

  • Supabase Inc.: Encrypted cloud authentication, row-level security (RLS), and database persistence.
  • Cloudflare R2 & Stream: Edge video distribution and global content delivery network (CDN).
  • Google & Meta OAuth: Federated identity authentication without storing third-party passwords.

6. Data Retention & Contact Us

We retain your data only for as long as your account remains active or as required by law. For privacy questions, rights requests, or Data Protection Officer inquiries, email: privacy@minsplay.com
