User Data Deletion

Compliance: Meta Platform Terms §4.5 & Google Play Safety Policy

Minsplay respects your right to be forgotten. You have full ownership of your data and may request account termination and permanent data destruction at any time.

Data Purged Upon Request

  • Identity & Credentials: Registered email, user ID, username, and social OAuth tokens.
  • Financial & Ledger Records: Coin balance, rewards streak, and in-app purchase receipts.
  • Playback & History: Watch progress timestamps, unlocked episode permissions, and favorites list.
  • Contributions: All episodic comments and likes posted on the platform.

Option 2: Meta App Settings Removal

If you signed in using Facebook, you can revoke permissions and initiate data removal directly from Meta:

  • Go to your Facebook account Settings & Privacy → Settings.
  • Navigate to Apps and Websites and find Minsplay.
  • Click Remove and check the box to request deletion of all data shared with Minsplay.

Option 3: Manual Email Request

You can also request manual data deletion by emailing our support team at support@minsplay.com with your registered email and Member ID. All manual requests are processed and verified within 48 to 72 business hours.
