Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Minsplay ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your personal data when you use our vertical short drama streaming platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Minsplay, we may collect the following data:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and avatar obtained via Google or Facebook OAuth.
  • Usage & Wallet: Video watch history, unlocked episodes, coin balances, and daily check-in streaks.
  • Interactions: Episodic comments, likes, and creator applications.
  • Device Analytics: IP address and device identifier for streaming performance.

2. How We Use Data

Your data is used strictly to authenticate sessions, persist coin balances across devices, deliver smooth video streaming, and moderate creator content.

3. Third-Party Infrastructure

  • Supabase: Secure cloud authentication and database persistence.
  • Cloudflare R2: Edge media CDN and video delivery.
